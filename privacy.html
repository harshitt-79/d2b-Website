This Privacy Policy explains how D2B – Design & Development for Business handles information in connection with this website and the enquiries and services that follow. It applies to visitors to this website, to anyone who contacts us through it, and to clients whose information we handle in the course of providing services. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

01Introduction

D2B – Design & Development for Business is a software and web development company based in Giridih, Jharkhand, India, providing website development, web applications and related digital business solutions to clients across India.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to explain, in clear terms, what information we may collect through this website and our communications, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to information shared with D2B through this website, through enquiry and contact forms, through email correspondence, and in the course of providing agreed services. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that may be linked from this website.

Note: this policy describes our current practices. Where a particular feature (such as newsletter subscription or website analytics) is not implemented on this website, the corresponding parts of this policy do not apply until that feature is enabled.

02Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with this website and with D2B, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details — such as your name, email address and phone number, when you provide them through a form or direct communication.
  • Business details — such as your business or organisation name, where relevant to an enquiry or project.
  • Service and project information — such as the service you are interested in, your requirements, project details, references, timelines and other information you choose to share.
  • Messages — the content of enquiries, requests, feedback and any correspondence you send us.
  • Technical information — such as your browser type, device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and general website usage information, where such information is collected through standard hosting logs or analytics.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information through this website, and we request that you do not send confidential credentials or sensitive documents through public forms. Please share only the information necessary for your enquiry.

03How We Collect Information

Information may reach us through one or more of the following channels:

  • Contact and enquiry forms on this website, where you choose to submit details about yourself and your requirements.
  • Newsletter subscription forms, where such a feature is implemented on the website.
  • Direct communication, such as email or telephone calls that you initiate with us.
  • Website analytics and cookies, where these are implemented, which may collect technical and usage information automatically.
  • Ongoing project communication, where you share additional information as part of an agreed engagement.

You are generally in control of the information you submit through forms and correspondence. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information so that we can respond appropriately.

04How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used for the following purposes:

  • responding to enquiries and requests for information;
  • understanding project requirements and preparing responses, proposals or quotations;
  • providing agreed services and communicating about ongoing work;
  • sending relevant project-related communication, updates and administrative messages;
  • improving the functionality, clarity and usability of this website;
  • maintaining the security and integrity of this website and our systems;
  • meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

We aim to use the information we collect only in ways that are consistent with the purpose for which it was provided, or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

05Email Communication

When you submit an enquiry or contact form, we may use the contact details you provide to respond to your enquiry, clarify your requirements and continue the conversation. These communications are directly related to your enquiry.

If a newsletter subscription feature is implemented on this website, subscribing indicates that you would like to receive relevant updates. Subscribers should be able to unsubscribe using the mechanism provided in those communications (for example, an unsubscribe link in the email), where such a mechanism is implemented. If you have subscribed and would like to stop receiving updates, you can also contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

We do not intend to send unrelated promotional messages to people who have not subscribed to receive them.

06Third-Party Services

Operating a website and communicating with visitors and clients may involve services provided by third parties. Where such services are used in connection with this website, they may process relevant information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Depending on what is implemented on this website at a given time, these may include:

  • Email delivery services — for example, an email delivery service such as EmailJS, where contact and enquiry forms are configured to use it. Information submitted through those forms may be transmitted through the provider's infrastructure in order to reach us.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — which operate the servers and services on which this website and related communications are hosted.
  • Analytics services — where analytics tools are implemented, they may process general usage information to help us understand how the website is used.
  • Other operational providers — such as domain, email or backup services, where these are necessary for the operation of the website.

Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own policies. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party services they choose to use.

07Cookies and Tracking

Cookies are small text files that a website may place on your device to support particular functions or to help understand how the website is used. Different types of cookies serve different purposes.

Where they are implemented on this website, cookies are generally used for one of the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — needed for core functions such as basic navigation and form operation.
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand aggregate website usage, where an analytics service is implemented.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or clear cookies; doing so may affect the availability or behaviour of some website features. Instructions are typically available in your browser's help section.

This policy does not claim that cookies, analytics or tracking technologies are used beyond what is actually implemented on this website at a given time.

08Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information that is shared with us, in line with the nature of the information and the way in which it is handled. This may include limiting access to information, using reputable service providers, and applying practical safeguards to the systems we use.

However, no method of transmission over the internet and no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. While we take reasonable care, we cannot promise that information shared with us will never be subject to unauthorised access or disclosure. Information is shared with us at your own discretion.

09Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business needs such as record keeping related to a project, or to meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we aim to delete or otherwise appropriately handle it, taking into account the nature of the information and any obligations that may require a longer retention period.

10Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the nature of the information and the applicable law, you may have certain rights in relation to your personal information. Where these apply, you may request to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of information, where legally applicable;
  • withdraw consent where consent was the basis for processing, subject to any applicable legal limitations;
  • ask for information about how your information is being used.

To make a request, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy. We aim to respond within a reasonable time. Some requests may be subject to verification or to limitations under applicable law.

Where applicable, we aim to handle personal data in a manner consistent with applicable Indian data-protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules and commencement provisions applicable to it, together with other applicable law. We do not claim compliance with any framework beyond what is actually applicable and implemented.

11Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business users and for adults seeking information about our services. It is not knowingly designed or directed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through this website, please contact us using the details below so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

12External Links

This website may contain links to external websites that are not operated by D2B. Those websites may have their own privacy policies and practices.

We are not responsible for the content, practices or privacy policies of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

13Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, our website or applicable requirements. When it is updated, the revised version will be published on this page.

The "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically so that you are aware of the current policy.
