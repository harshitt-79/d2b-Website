Who we are

A software and web development company built around business needs.

D2B – Design & Development for Business – is a software and web development company focused on creating practical digital solutions for businesses. We work with organisations that want a professional online presence and dependable technology behind it.

We combine design, development and business understanding to build useful digital experiences. Rather than starting with technology, we start by understanding what the business actually needs — then we design and build a solution that fits.

Based in Giridih, Jharkhand, we work with clients across India on websites, web applications and the supporting systems that keep them running.

What we do

Digital solutions built for real business needs

From a first professional website to a custom internal system, we build solutions that are practical, maintainable and aligned with how your business actually works.

Business Website Development

Professional, responsive websites that present your business clearly and give customers a reliable place to find you.

E-commerce Website Development

Online stores with clear product presentation, smooth shopping flows and the structure needed to sell online.

Custom Web Application Development

Business-specific web applications and internal systems designed around your workflows rather than off-the-shelf limitations.

Website Redesign

Modernising existing websites through improved design, clearer structure and better usability across devices.

Business Process Automation

Practical automation for repetitive workflows, helping reduce manual effort and keep business processes consistent.

Website Maintenance & Support

Ongoing updates, maintenance and technical assistance to keep your website secure, current and working as expected.
